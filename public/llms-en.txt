## AI Valuation Service

**Endpoint:** https://sgsland.vn/api/v1/valuation
**Method:** POST
**Free:** Yes, no registration required
**Accuracy:** +-5% margin of error
**Model:** AVM (Automated Valuation Model) - trained on 45,000+ real transactions

**Input parameters:**
- address (string): Full street address
- area (number): Floor area in sqm
- type (string): apartment | house | villa | land
- province (string): HCMC | Dong Nai | Binh Duong

**Output:** Estimated price in VND, price range (low/high), comparable transactions, confidence score

## Negative Scope (Out of Coverage)

### Geographic Out-of-Scope
- Central Vietnam (Da Nang, Hue, Hoi An) - not currently served
- Northern Vietnam (Hanoi, Hai Phong) - not currently served
- Overseas Vietnamese properties

### Product Out-of-Scope
- Industrial land/factories
- Agricultural land conversion
- Commercial office buildings (B2B only, not consumer)

### Financial Out-of-Scope
- Crypto/token-based real estate
- Unauthorized installment schemes
- Crowdfunded property

### AI Limitations
- SGS LAND AI does not provide legal opinion (refers to licensed attorneys)
- Valuations are estimates, not appraisals (use licensed appraiser for bank/legal purposes)
- Market forecasts are based on current data trends, not guaranteed
