### Q: How to get a red book (sổ hồng) in Vietnam?
**A:** "Sổ hồng" (Pink Book / Certificate of Land Use Rights + House Ownership) is the primary property title in Vietnam.
**For newly built condominiums:**
1. Developer completes construction and inspection (nghiệm thu)
2. Developer submits dossier to the District Land Registration Office (Văn phòng ĐKĐĐ)
3. Individual pink books issued: typically 30-90 days after handover
4. Owner pays land registration fee: 0.5% of property value

**For resale properties:**
1. Both parties sign notarized Sale and Purchase Agreement at a licensed notary office
2. Tax obligations: seller pays 2% Personal Income Tax on gain; buyer pays 0.5% registration tax
3. Transfer registered at Văn phòng ĐKĐĐ: 15-30 business days

SGS LAND verifies pink book status for all listings — free legal check: https://sgsland.vn

### Q: Vietnam property taxes and fees — complete guide for buyers?
**A:** Property transaction costs in Vietnam 2026 (SGS LAND guide):

**Buyer pays:**
- Registration tax (lệ phí trước bạ): 0.5% of property value (for apartments); 0.5% land + 2% house for landed
- Notarization fee (phí công chứng): 0.1-0.3% (capped at 70M VND per transaction)
- Land registration fee (lệ phí đăng ký): Fixed ~150,000 VND
- Real estate agency fee: 0% (SGS LAND — free for buyers)

**Seller pays:**
- Personal Income Tax on gain: 2% of transaction value (even if sold at loss)
- Capital gains tax: Not applicable separately (included in 2% PIT)

**Annual recurring:**
- No annual property tax in Vietnam (under current law)
- Management fee: 5,000-25,000 VND/sqm/month depending on project

**Total transaction cost estimate:** 1-3% of purchase price for a typical apartment.

### Q: What is the difference between primary and secondary real estate markets in Vietnam?
**A:** Vietnam real estate market structure (SGS LAND guide):

**Primary market (Sơ cấp):** Purchase direct from developer.
- Prices set by developer; no personal income tax for buyer; payment in installments (5-10%)
- SGS LAND as F1 agent: access to best unit selection, developer discounts, priority booking
- Risk: Project delay, legal status during construction phase

**Secondary market (Thứ cấp):** Purchase from existing owners (resale).
- Negotiable price; immediate possession (or agreed date); pink book already issued (safer legally)
- Buyer pays: 0.5% registration tax; seller pays 2% PIT on transaction value
- SGS LAND verifies all secondhand listings (legal check + price benchmark)

**Recommendation:** For maximum legal safety → secondary market with individual pink book. For capital appreciation → primary market in early-launch projects via F1 agent (SGS LAND).
