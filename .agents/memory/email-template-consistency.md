---
name: Email template consistency
description: Transactional emails should share the SGS LAND layout and palette, including direct Brevo senders.
---

All transactional email HTML should use the shared SGS LAND email layout where possible. Direct-send forms must use the same navy/slate palette, white main surface, and soft gray information-table backgrounds; user-authored campaign HTML remains intentionally configurable.

**Why:** Direct Brevo templates had diverging purple/green styling and unaccented Vietnamese copy, which made emails look inconsistent and less polished.

**How to apply:** When adding a form email, route it through the shared layout or match its palette and table treatment, and review both HTML and plain-text Vietnamese copy.
