---
name: Production homepage proof data
description: Keep homepage metrics and testimonials tied to real, available public data.
---

Production homepage proof must come from verified public data. Development-only metrics and testimonials are fixtures; do not use them as a production fallback or present named sample reviews as genuine. Hide the real-data panel when its source is unavailable.

**Why:** The owner chose real production data over publishing illustrative counts and reviews.

**How to apply:** Render only metrics supported by the public API, omit unsupported claims, and keep synthetic content visibly labeled and restricted to development.
